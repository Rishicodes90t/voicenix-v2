How we think about sound

Not every line needs
to be louder.

Sometimes a line needs room to breathe, a softer take, or a different pause. Good audio begins by listening for what the scene is trying to say.

From our base in Subhash Nagar, New Delhi, we work with creators and production teams on scripted series, voice-over, music and more. Start with a rough brief; we will work through the right setup together.

Some lines need energy. Others need a little silence around them.

What guides us

Three habits,
every session.

01

Listen first

Before any edit or effect, we listen for the feeling you want people to leave with.

02

Room to breathe

Patient takes, comfortable pauses and a room where performers can take their time.

03

Details that feel true

Breath, timing, tone and finish: the small things that make a line believable.
