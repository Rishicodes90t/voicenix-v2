VOICENIX STUDIOS — HUMAN / ANALOGUE SCROLL EDITION

QUICK START
1. Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js and the assets folder together in the published folder.
2. For local preview, open index.html, or run: python -m http.server 8000
3. For GitHub Pages, upload the CONTENTS of this folder to your existing repository root (not the enclosing folder). Choose Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root).

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Warm studio palette: espresso, oxblood, parchment, copper and champagne; violet/neon effects removed from the main experience.
- The opening uses a photographed vocal booth, one large editorial V and a fine animated waveform rather than floating music notes, stars or a morphing blob.
- “Voicenix” is kept fully legible. A fine headphone arc sits around the real “o”; one “i” has a small microphone-inspired tittle, while the other “i” remains a normal letter to keep the wordmark balanced.
- The studio/people section appears immediately after Services, before the portfolio, and copy centres on listening, performance, breath, pause and the details that make a line feel true.
- The burgundy opening is the permanent first section of the page. It never disappears or auto-closes; visitors can scroll naturally into the announcement, navigation and rest of the website. “Explore the studio,” “Book a session,” and “Hear a sample” links jump to relevant sections.
- Motion is intentionally restrained: a single waveform draws on, the service ribbon moves slowly, and interactive elements have simple feedback. No sound autoplay.

FILES AND CONTENT
- assets/weekend-offer-poster.jpg is the poster supplied for the project.
- Studio images are bundled locally in assets/. Use genuine photos of your actual room, talent and engineer wherever possible; the supplied artwork is illustrative.
- Included audio files are synthetic interface demos, not real client recordings, endorsements or evidence of production quality. Replace them with approved studio samples before publishing as a portfolio.
- Portfolio project titles are concept studies and should be replaced with approved work as available.
- The booking form prepares a WhatsApp message to +91 98705 32587; it does not store enquiries on a server.
- Confirm contact details, address, discount terms, language availability and talent availability before publishing.
- Google Fonts are used when online; fallback fonts are included.
