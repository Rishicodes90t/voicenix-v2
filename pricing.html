Good to know

A few things
before we record.

Every project is a little different. Here are the basics to help you start.

What should I share before asking for a quote?+

Send your script or a rough duration, the kind of project, language or accent requirements, delivery deadline and any sound references you have. A rough brief is absolutely fine for the first conversation.

Do you work on dubbing for short dramas and OTT content?+

Short-drama and screen-content dubbing are part of the services this site is built around. Share the episode count, duration, language pair and sync requirements so we can confirm the right scope and talent.

Can I book a weekend recording session?+

Yes, enquiries are welcome. The 20% offer applies to confirmed weekend studio-session fees, subject to availability. Mention WEEKEND20 when you contact us.

How do revisions and delivery work?+

We agree on deliverables, file formats and the included revision scope before work begins. Larger script changes or extra versions may require a revised quote.

Where is the studio?+

We are at 12/51 Subhash Nagar, New Delhi – 110027. Sessions are by prior enquiry, so message us first and we will confirm the slot and share directions.

Which languages can you record in?+

We regularly work with Hindi, English, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Punjabi and Malayalam. Other languages and accents are possible on request; we confirm talent availability for your language pair before booking.

Can I join the session remotely?+

Ask us when you enquire. Remote direction and review depend on the project and setup, and we will tell you plainly what is possible.

Can I book if my script is not final yet?+

Of course. You can discuss your idea first. Before the actual recording, a locked or near-final script helps avoid retakes and keeps the project on schedule.
